# CET-6 近十年写作与翻译范文高频表达汇编（2016—2025）

> **整理日期：2026-10-17**  
> **用途：**
> 六级作文、汉译英翻译的可调用表达库；重点解决“认识单词，但写作时想不起来、不会搭配”的问题。  
> **资料范围：** 2016—2025
> 年公开可查的六级写作题目、培训机构参考范文、历年翻译题目与参考译文；同时整合已有《英语六级作文翻译词块整理（扩充版）》中的词块，如职业准备、数字素养、社会实践、文化科技翻译词汇等。

## 先说明：这份资料里的“答案”和“范文”是什么

- **写作没有唯一标准答案。**
  公开网页上的范文通常由新东方、沪江、文都、考试网站或其他英语教学平台整理，是“参考范文”，不是考试委员会发布的官方标准作文。
- **翻译也常有多个参考译文。**
  只要准确传达原文信息、语法正确、表达自然，不必逐字照搬某一个版本。
- 本资料不把十年所有范文整篇复制进来，而是从公开真题与参考范文中提炼可迁移的句架、词块和常见搭配；少量短语摘录会注明来源。其余例句为教学改写或归纳句，不冒充原文。
- 年份主题表用于帮助你了解题材范围，**属于代表性主题索引，不是每一套试卷的穷尽清单**。同一考试可能有多套卷，考后回忆版也可能存在细节差异。

### 从十年题材里能看出的复用方向

1.  **个人成长：** improve skills, develop good habits, build
    confidence, gain experience, prepare for future challenges。
2.  **社会关系：** respect others, understand different perspectives,
    help those in need, strengthen mutual trust, fulfill
    responsibilities。
3.  **科技与风险：** make use of technology, improve efficiency, protect
    privacy, avoid over-reliance, think independently。
4.  **实践与教育：** put knowledge into practice, gain hands-on
    experience, develop problem-solving skills, adapt to real-world
    needs。
5.  **中国文化与发展：** have a long history, be known for, reflect
    cultural values, promote economic development, improve public
    services。
6.  **问题解决：** identify the problem, take practical measures,
    address the issue, reduce potential risks, evaluate the results。

**复习原则：**
不要按年份背十篇作文。按“表达功能”整理更划算：同一个“提高能力”的表达，可以用于数字素养、职业准备、团队合作、社会实践、独立思考等多种主题。

## 2. 范文里最值得积累的好句

下面分为“表达功能—句架—适用情境”。句架中的方括号内容要替换成题目具体信息，不能不看题目就整句硬套。

### 2.1 开头：引出话题、描述趋势

1.  **There is growing recognition of the importance of \[topic\].**  
    人们越来越认识到……的重要性。适合能力、教育、责任、数字素养等话题。

2.  **In recent years, \[topic\] has become increasingly important in
    \[field\].**  
    近年来，……在……中变得越来越重要。比单独写 Nowadays 信息更具体。

3.  **As \[technology/practice\] becomes more common, its benefits and
    potential risks deserve attention.**  
    随着……越来越普遍，其好处和潜在风险值得关注。适合
    AI、社交媒体、在线学习。

4.  **When it comes to \[topic\], people may hold different views.**  
    谈到……，人们可能有不同看法。适合观点选择题，但后面必须明确自己的立场。

5.  **In a rapidly changing world, the ability to \[do something\] has
    become increasingly valuable.**  
    在快速变化的世界里，……的能力变得越来越有价值。适合终身学习、适应变化、职业准备。

6.  **The question is not whether we should use \[something\], but how
    we can use it wisely.**  
    问题不在于是否使用……，而在于如何明智地使用。适合
    AI、网络、数字工具。

7.  **The chart shows a marked change in \[figure/category\] from
    \[year\] to \[year\].**  
    图表显示……从某年到某年发生了明显变化。图表作文可按真实数据替换。

8.  **The figures suggest that considerable progress has been made in
    \[area\].**  
    数据表明，……领域已经取得了相当大的进展。只有图表确实支持“进步”时才能用。

### 2.2 表态：说明自己的观点

9.  **From my perspective, \[position\] is a practical choice for
    \[group\].**  
    在我看来，……对……来说是一个切实可行的选择。

10. **I believe that the benefits of \[practice\] depend largely on how
    it is used.**  
    我认为，……的好处在很大程度上取决于它如何被使用。适合科技、社交媒体等双面性话题。

11. **Rather than treating \[A\] and \[B\] as opposites, we should learn
    to balance them.**  
    与其把 A 和 B
    视为对立面，不如学会平衡二者。适合学习与课外活动、工作与休闲、AI
    与独立思考。

12. **Although \[A\] has clear advantages, it should not be regarded as
    a complete solution to \[B\].**  
    尽管 A 有明显优势，但不应把它视为解决 B 的完整方案。

13. **The key is to make informed decisions rather than follow a trend
    blindly.**  
    关键是作出有依据的决定，而不是盲目跟风。适合追星、网络信息、消费与技术使用。

### 2.3 展开理由：让观点有内容

14. **One major reason is that \[specific reason\].**  
    一个主要原因是……。比反复使用 The reason is that 更灵活。

15. **More importantly, \[point\] can help people \[result\].**  
    更重要的是，……能帮助人们……。

16. **This is because \[explanation\].**  
    这是因为……。适合接着解释前一句，而不是单独用来凑字数。

17. **By \[doing something\], students can develop \[skill/quality\] and
    become better prepared for \[goal\].**  
    通过……，学生能够培养……，并更好地为……做准备。

18. **Such experience helps turn theoretical knowledge into practical
    skills.**  
    这样的经历有助于把理论知识转化为实践技能。适合实习、项目、社会实践。

19. **It not only \[benefit A\] but also \[benefit B\].**  
    它不仅……，而且……。注意 not only 和 but also
    后面尽量保持语法结构平行。

20. **This enables \[person/group\] to \[action\], which in turn helps
    \[result\].**  
    这使……能够……，进而有助于……。适合解释“措施如何带来结果”。

21. **For example, students who take part in \[activity\] may learn to
    \[action\].**  
    例如，参加……的学生可能学会……。例子要具体，不能只写 Students can
    benefit a lot。

22. **My own experience has taught me that \[lesson\].**  
    我自己的经历让我明白……。只在题目允许结合个人经历、且能举出真实或合理例子时使用。

### 2.4 好处：从抽象评价转向具体结果

23. **It can improve the quality of \[life/education/services\].**  
    它能改善……的质量。

24. **It makes it easier for people to \[do something\].**  
    它让人们更容易……。简单、稳定，适合基础薄弱时使用。

25. **It helps broaden people’s horizons and deepen their understanding
    of \[topic\].**  
    它有助于开阔视野并加深对……的理解。

26. **It provides students with opportunities to gain hands-on
    experience.**  
    它为学生提供获得实践经验的机会。

27. **It can strengthen cooperation and build mutual trust.**  
    它能加强合作并建立相互信任。

28. **It lays a solid foundation for \[future
    study/career/development\].**  
    它为……打下坚实基础。

29. **In the long run, these efforts can bring lasting benefits to
    \[group/society\].**  
    从长远来看，这些努力能为……带来持久的好处。

### 2.5 缺点与风险：别只会写 bad

30. **However, \[practice\] may also create problems if it is used
    improperly.**  
    然而，如果使用不当，……也可能带来问题。

31. **Excessive reliance on \[technology/person\] may weaken people’s
    ability to \[action\].**  
    过度依赖……可能削弱人们……的能力。

32. **One potential drawback is that \[problem\].**  
    一个潜在缺点是……。

33. **If left unchecked, this problem may lead to \[consequence\].**  
    如果不加以控制，这个问题可能导致……。

34. **The benefits should be weighed against the possible risks.**  
    应当权衡好处与潜在风险。

35. **Convenience should not come at the expense of
    \[privacy/independence/health\].**  
    不能以牺牲……为代价换取便利。

36. **This does not mean that we should reject \[technology/practice\]
    altogether.**  
    这并不意味着我们应该完全拒绝……。适合提出平衡观点。

### 2.6 实践与措施：写清楚“谁做什么”

37. **Students should put what they have learned into practice.**  
    学生应该把所学知识付诸实践。

38. **Universities can provide more opportunities for students to
    participate in internships and projects.**  
    高校可以为学生提供更多参与实习和项目的机会。

39. **Instead of relying on slogans, we should take practical measures
    to address the problem.**  
    我们应该采取切实措施解决问题，而不是只喊口号。

40. **Parents should guide children while allowing them enough room to
    make decisions on their own.**  
    家长应给予指导，同时让孩子有足够空间自主作决定。

41. **Schools can help students develop critical thinking by encouraging
    them to ask questions and evaluate evidence.**  
    学校可以通过鼓励学生提问、评估证据来培养批判性思维。

42. **Users should protect their privacy and verify information before
    sharing it online.**  
    用户应保护隐私，并在网上分享信息前核实其真实性。

43. **The government and the public should work together to
    \[goal\].**  
    政府和公众应共同努力实现……。使用前要确认题目确实涉及这两个主体。

44. **Clear goals, regular practice and timely feedback can make
    improvement more achievable.**  
    明确的目标、持续练习和及时反馈能让进步更容易实现。

### 2.7 结尾：总结并落到行动

45. **In conclusion, \[topic\] matters because it affects both \[A\] and
    \[B\].**  
    总之，……很重要，因为它同时影响……和……。

46. **Therefore, we should focus not only on recognizing the problem but
    also on taking action.**  
    因此，我们不仅要认识问题，还要采取行动。

47. **With clear goals and consistent effort, people can make steady
    progress.**  
    有了明确目标和持续努力，人们就能稳步进步。

48. **The goal is not to avoid every difficulty, but to develop the
    ability to deal with it.**  
    目标不是避免所有困难，而是培养应对困难的能力。

49. **If used wisely and responsibly, \[technology/practice\] can serve
    as a valuable tool rather than a source of problems.**  
    如果明智、负责任地使用，……可以成为有价值的工具，而不是问题的来源。

50. **Ultimately, meaningful progress depends on turning good intentions
    into concrete action.**  
    归根结底，真正的进步取决于把良好愿望转化为具体行动。

## 3. 高频词同义替换总表

> **重要提醒：同义替换不是把词随便互换。**
> 先看中文要表达的是“改善现状、增强能力、促进发展、提高数字”中的哪一种，再选择合适的动词。下面每组都给了常见搭配和语义区别。

| 中文核心意思     | 常见英文替换                                                                                  | 常见搭配                                                                                                                                   | 使用区别                                                                                                                              |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 提高、改善       | improve, enhance, boost, raise, increase, strengthen, develop                                 | improve skills/quality; enhance ability/awareness; boost confidence; raise standards; increase efficiency; strengthen ties; develop skills | improve 最通用；enhance 偏提升质量/能力；boost 强调显著提振；raise/increase 多接数量、水平；strengthen 强调变强；develop 强调逐步培养 |
| 进步、发展       | progress, advance, development, growth, improvement                                           | make progress; technological advancement; economic development; personal growth                                                            | progress 常作不可数名词；development 可指发展过程或领域；advancement 较正式，常用于科技、事业                                         |
| 促进、推动       | promote, encourage, foster, facilitate, advance, drive                                        | promote cooperation; encourage creativity; foster independence; facilitate communication; drive innovation                                 | promote 推动发展/推广；encourage 鼓励某人做事；foster 培养较长期的品质；facilitate 使过程更容易；drive 推动变革或增长                 |
| 好处、益处       | benefit, advantage, merit, positive effect, value, gain                                       | bring benefits; have advantages; the merits of; positive effects on; practical value                                                       | benefit 适用最广；advantage 强调相对优势；merit 偏优点/价值；value 强调用途或价值                                                     |
| 有益的、有帮助的 | beneficial, helpful, useful, valuable, advantageous                                           | be beneficial to; be helpful in doing; a useful tool; valuable experience                                                                  | beneficial to 后接名词/代词；helpful in 后常接名词或动名词；valuable experience 表示宝贵经验                                          |
| 缺点、弊端       | drawback, disadvantage, downside, limitation, weakness, shortcoming                           | a major drawback; the downside of; practical limitations; personal weaknesses                                                              | drawback/downside 常指负面后果；disadvantage 指不利条件；limitation 指局限；weakness 指弱点                                           |
| 问题、难题       | problem, issue, challenge, difficulty, obstacle, concern                                      | address an issue; tackle a problem; overcome difficulties; pose a challenge; raise concerns                                                | issue 更中性/正式；challenge 是需要努力应对的挑战，不完全等于“问题”；obstacle 是阻碍；concern 是令人担忧之处                          |
| 解决、应对       | solve, address, tackle, deal with, cope with, overcome, resolve                               | solve a problem; address the issue; tackle the challenge; deal with pressure; cope with stress; overcome obstacles; resolve a conflict     | solve 常用于有明确答案的问题；address/tackle 常用于社会问题；cope with 强调应付；overcome 强调克服障碍；resolve 常用于冲突/争议       |
| 实践、实际应用   | practice, practice/practical experience, application, implementation, hands-on work           | put theory into practice; practical experience; practical application; implementation of a policy; hands-on training                       | practice 作名词可指实践/练习；practical 是形容词；application 是应用；implementation 是政策/计划的落实                                |
| 提高效率         | improve efficiency, enhance efficiency, increase productivity, streamline a process           | improve work efficiency; enhance operational efficiency; increase productivity; streamline procedures                                      | efficiency 指效率；productivity 指单位时间产出；streamline 强调简化流程                                                               |
| 培养、发展能力   | develop, cultivate, foster, build, strengthen, acquire                                        | develop skills; cultivate good habits; foster independence; build confidence; acquire knowledge                                            | develop 通用；cultivate 常接习惯/素养；foster 常接独立性/创造力；build 常接信心/关系；acquire 常接知识/技能                           |
| 重要、关键       | important, essential, crucial, vital, significant, key                                        | play an important role; be essential to; a crucial factor; of vital importance; significant progress; the key to success                   | important 最稳；essential/crucial/vital 强调不可或缺；significant 常表示重要/显著；key 常作名词或形容词                               |
| 重要性           | importance, significance, value, role                                                         | recognize the importance of; the significance of; practical value; play a role in                                                          | importance 通用；significance 更正式；value 强调价值；role 强调作用                                                                   |
| 帮助             | help, assist, support, enable, allow, facilitate                                              | help sb do; assist sb with; support students; enable sb to do; allow sb to do; facilitate learning                                         | help 最通用；assist 正式；support 表示支持；enable 强调使某事成为可能；allow 表示允许/使得                                            |
| 影响             | affect, influence, have an impact on, shape, determine                                        | affect behavior; influence decisions; have an impact on; shape one’s future; determine the outcome                                         | affect 常作动词；influence 可作动词/名词；impact 常用于较明显的影响；shape 强调塑造；determine 强调决定性作用                         |

| 使用、利用       | use, make use of, make full use of, utilize, employ, apply, adopt                             | use technology; make full use of resources; utilize data; apply a method; adopt a strategy                                                 | use 最稳；utilize 更正式但不必滥用；apply 强调应用方法/技术于某领域；adopt 强调采纳方法/政策                                          |
| 实现、达到       | achieve, attain, reach, accomplish, realize, fulfill                                          | achieve a goal; attain a standard; reach an agreement; accomplish a task; realize a dream; fulfill a responsibility                        | achieve a goal/result；reach a level/agreement；accomplish a task；realize a dream/ambition；fulfill a duty/requirement               |
| 增加、增长       | increase, rise, grow, expand, climb, surge                                                    | increase significantly; the number rises; the economy grows; expand access; figures climb; demand surges                                   | increase 可及物/不及物；rise 不及物；expand 强调范围扩大；surge 指突然大幅增长                                                        |
| 减少、降低       | reduce, decrease, lower, cut, decline, minimize                                               | reduce costs; decrease pollution; lower risks; cut emissions; the number declines; minimize errors                                         | reduce/decrease 通用；lower 常接水平/风险；cut 常接成本/排放；decline 多描述趋势；minimize 是尽可能降低                               |

## 4. 重点词群：实践、好处、缺点、提高

### 4.1 “实践”：别只记 practice 一个词

| 表达                     | 中文意思           | 适用场景                     | 例句                                                                                   |
|--------------------------|--------------------|------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| practice                 | 实践；练习         | 一般实践、反复练习           | Regular practice helps students improve their writing.                                 |
| put theory into practice | 把理论付诸实践     | 学习、课程、职业准备         | Internships help students put classroom knowledge into practice.                       |
| practical experience     | 实践经验           | 实习、工作、项目             | Practical experience is valuable when students apply for jobs.                         |
| hands-on experience      | 亲自动手获得的经验 | 实验、项目、实习、技术训练   | Project work gives students hands-on experience in solving real problems.              |
| practical skills         | 实用技能/实践技能  | 职业准备、就业能力           | Students should develop practical skills before graduation.                            |
| social practice          | 社会实践           | 志愿服务、社区活动、社会调研 | Social practice allows students to understand society beyond the classroom.            |
| practical application    | 实际应用           | 科技、理论、知识             | The practical application of new technology can improve public services.               |
| implementation           | 实施、落实         | 政策、计划、措施、方案       | The successful implementation of a policy requires clear goals and regular evaluation. |
| carry out a plan         | 执行计划           | 行动建议                     | Schools should carry out the plan and evaluate its results.                            |
| take practical measures  | 采取切实措施       | 问题解决型作文               | The government should take practical measures to reduce pollution.                     |

**怎么选：** - “社会实践活动”通常写 **social practice activities**
或更具体的 **volunteer work/community projects**。 -
“把理论知识用于真实场景”写 **put/apply theoretical knowledge into
practice**。 - “政策实施”优先用 **implementation of a policy**，不要写
*practice a policy*。 - “亲自操作的经验”写 **hands-on
experience**，比单独写 *practice experience* 自然。

### 4.2 “好处”：把 benefit 展开成具体结果

| 表达                         | 重点                   | 例句                                                                    |
|------------------------------|------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| benefit                      | 好处、益处；也可作动词 | Students can benefit from regular feedback.                             |
| advantage                    | 优势，常用于比较       | One advantage of online learning is its flexibility.                    |
| positive effect/impact       | 积极影响               | Volunteer work can have a positive impact on the community.             |
| practical value              | 实用价值               | Digital literacy has practical value in both study and work.            |
| bring benefits to            | 给……带来好处           | Better public transport brings benefits to local residents.             |
| be beneficial to             | 对……有益               | Regular exercise is beneficial to both physical and mental well-being.  |
| contribute to                | 有助于、促成           | Teamwork contributes to better problem-solving.                         |
| make it easier for sb. to do | 让某人更容易做某事     | Digital tools make it easier for students to access learning materials. |
| broaden one’s horizons       | 开阔视野               | Reading widely broadens students’ horizons.                             |
| build/boost confidence       | 建立/增强信心          | Completing a difficult project can boost students’ confidence.          |
| improve quality of life      | 改善生活质量           | Reliable public services can improve people’s quality of life.          |
| lay a solid foundation for   | 为……打基础             | Basic knowledge lays a solid foundation for further learning.           |

**写作升级法：** 不要只写 *It has many benefits.* 要具体写：

- It **improves efficiency** by reducing repetitive work.
- It **broadens students’ horizons** by exposing them to different
  perspectives.
- It **builds confidence** because students can see the results of their
  own efforts.
- It **lays a solid foundation for future career development**.

### 4.3 “缺点”：从 bad 进阶到风险表达

| 表达                   | 中文意思       | 常见搭配/例句                                                                 |
|------------------------|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| disadvantage           | 不利条件、劣势 | One disadvantage of online learning is the lack of face-to-face interaction.  |
| drawback               | 缺点、弊端     | A major drawback is that users may become overly dependent on technology.     |
| downside               | 负面的一面     | The downside of convenience is the risk of sharing personal information.      |
| limitation             | 局限           | This approach has limitations when students need immediate feedback.          |
| risk                   | 风险           | Excessive screen time may pose a risk to children’s well-being.               |
| potential harm         | 潜在危害       | We should consider the potential harm of spreading unverified information.    |
| over-reliance on       | 对……过度依赖   | Over-reliance on AI may weaken independent thinking.                          |
| undermine              | 削弱、损害     | Constant interruptions can undermine concentration.                           |
| weaken                 | 削弱           | Copying answers may weaken students’ ability to solve problems independently. |
| come at the expense of | 以牺牲……为代价 | Efficiency should not come at the expense of privacy.                         |
| do more harm than good | 弊大于利       | Excessive protection may do more harm than good.                              |
| if left unchecked      | 如果不加控制   | If left unchecked, false information can spread rapidly.                      |

**不要把所有负面词当成一个意思：** -
**drawback/downside**：说某事的缺点。 -
**risk**：说某事可能造成的危险。 -
**limitation**：说某方法不能解决什么，或适用范围有限。 -
**undermine/weaken**：直接说“削弱了什么能力/信任/效果”。 - **lead
to/result in**：描述负面结果。

### 4.4 “提高”：improve、enhance、boost、promote 不能随便换

| 词             | 核心用法                                   | 高概率搭配                                                              | 例句                                                                      |
|----------------|--------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **improve**    | 改善现状、使质量/能力变好；最通用          | improve skills, performance, quality, efficiency, living conditions     | Regular practice can improve students’ writing skills.                    |
| **enhance**    | 提升某种能力、质量、价值、体验，语气较正式 | enhance ability, awareness, understanding, experience, security         | Reading different sources can enhance our understanding of social issues. |
| **boost**      | 明显提振、增强，常用于信心、士气、增长     | boost confidence, motivation, productivity, economic growth             | Positive feedback can boost learners’ confidence.                         |
| **promote**    | 促进发展、推广某理念/活动                  | promote cooperation, health, cultural exchange, sustainable development | Social practice can promote cooperation among students.                   |
| **raise**      | 提高数值、标准、意识或提出问题             | raise awareness, standards, concerns, questions                         | Schools should raise students’ awareness of online safety.                |
| **increase**   | 增加数量、比例、水平                       | increase efficiency, income, access, participation                      | Online resources can increase access to learning materials.               |
| **strengthen** | 加强原本存在的能力、关系、体系             | strengthen skills, ties, cooperation, management                        | Team projects can strengthen communication and cooperation.               |
| **develop**    | 逐步发展、培养能力/习惯/系统               | develop skills, habits, critical thinking, a system                     | Students should develop the habit of checking information carefully.      |
| **advance**    | 推进某领域发展、使其前进                   | advance technology, scientific research, social progress                | Innovation can advance scientific research.                               |
| **upgrade**    | 升级设备、设施、系统、水平                 | upgrade facilities, equipment, infrastructure                           | Cities need to upgrade public transport facilities.                       |
| **refine**     | 打磨、改进细节或想法                       | refine ideas, methods, skills                                           | Feedback helps students refine their ideas.                               |
| **maximize**   | 使……最大化                                 | maximize benefits, efficiency, opportunities                            | We should use digital tools to maximize learning opportunities.           |

**快速记忆：** - 提高写作能力：**improve writing skills** -
提升安全意识：**raise/enhance awareness of safety** - 增强信心：**boost
confidence** - 促进合作：**promote cooperation** -
加强关系：**strengthen relationships/ties** -
培养独立思考：**develop/foster independent thinking** -
推动科技进步：**advance technological progress** -
改善生活质量：**improve the quality of life**

## 5. 作文常用词块：原因、结果、建议、例子、结尾

### 5.1 论证原因

- one major reason is that … 一个主要原因是……
- this can be explained by … 这可以用……解释
- the main factor behind this trend is … 这一趋势背后的主要因素是……
- this is largely because … 这主要是因为……
- owing to / due to … 由于……（后接名词/名词短语）
- as a result of … 由于……；作为……的结果
- be closely related to … 与……密切相关
- stem from … 源于……
- be driven by … 由……推动

例句：

- The growing demand for digital skills is closely related to changes in
  the workplace.
- This problem partly stems from a lack of reliable information.
- The trend is driven by both technological progress and changing social
  needs.

### 5.2 论证结果

- as a result / consequently 因此、结果
- in turn 进而、反过来
- thereby + V-ing 从而……
- lead to / result in 导致
- contribute to 促成、有助于
- make a difference 产生影响
- have a lasting impact on 对……产生持久影响
- bring about changes 带来变化

例句：

- Learning to evaluate evidence can improve decision-making and, in
  turn, reduce the risk of being misled.
- Better transport links can shorten travel time, thereby making
  regional cooperation easier.
- A supportive environment can make a real difference to students’
  confidence.

### 5.3 举例与个人经历

- for example / for instance 例如
- take … as an example 以……为例
- a case in point is … 一个典型例子是……
- this can be seen in … 这可以从……看出
- in my own experience 在我自己的经历中
- I once had the opportunity to … 我曾有机会……
- what I learned from this experience was that … 我从这次经历中学到……

**例子写法：** 人物/场景 + 具体动作 + 结果。不要只写 *For example, it is
very important.*

- During a group project, I learned to divide tasks clearly and listen
  to different opinions. As a result, our team completed the work more
  efficiently.
- Through volunteer work, students can learn to communicate with people
  from different backgrounds and better understand local needs.

### 5.4 建议与解决问题

- take effective/practical measures 采取有效/切实措施
- address the root cause 解决根本原因
- tackle the problem step by step 一步步处理问题
- set clear and realistic goals 设定清晰、现实的目标
- establish clear rules 制定明确规则
- provide guidance and support 提供指导和支持
- raise public awareness 提高公众意识
- encourage active participation 鼓励积极参与
- strengthen supervision 加强监督
- evaluate the results 定期评估结果
- strike a balance between A and B 平衡 A 和 B
- make informed decisions 作出有依据的决定

例句：

- To address this issue, schools should teach students how to evaluate
  online information instead of simply telling them to stay away from
  the Internet.
- Parents can provide guidance while allowing children to make
  age-appropriate decisions on their own.
- The best approach is to strike a balance between the convenience of
  technology and the need for independent thinking.

### 5.5 连接词：按逻辑选择，不要堆砌

| 逻辑           | 可用表达                                        | 注意                                       |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| 开始第一个理由 | To begin with; One major reason is that …       | 不必每篇都写 First and foremost            |
| 添加理由       | Moreover; In addition; Besides; What is more    | 一个段落中选择一两个即可                   |
| 强调重点       | More importantly; Above all                     | 后面应真的是更重要的内容                   |
| 举例           | For example; For instance; Take … as an example | 例子要与论点直接相关                       |
| 转折           | However; Nevertheless; Yet                      | 不要同一句里无必要地重复转折               |
| 让步           | Although; Even though; Admittedly               | Although 后接完整句；通常不要再加 but      |
| 结果           | Therefore; Consequently; As a result            | 结果必须能由前文推出                       |
| 总结           | In conclusion; To sum up; Overall               | 结尾应总结立场或提出行动，不要重复三遍观点 |

## 6. 翻译高频动词：把中文意思变成英文句子

### 6.5 常见中文句式的自然表达

- 随着……的发展：**With the development of …** / **As … develops, …**
- 随着生活水平的提高：**As living standards improve, …**
- 位于中国东部/西南部：**be located in eastern/southwestern China**
- 有悠久历史：**have a long history**
- 是……之一：**be one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词**
- 不仅……而且……：**not only … but also …**
- 使某人能够做某事：**enable sb. to do sth.**
- 使做某事成为可能：**make it possible to do sth.**
- 由于……，……：**Due to … , …** / **Because … , …**
- 这使得……：**This makes it possible for … to …**
- 在过去几十年里：**Over the past few decades, …**
- 近年来：**In recent years, …**
- 至今仍在使用：**remain in use today** / **still be in use today**
- 对……产生积极影响：**have a positive impact on …**
- 在……方面取得显著进展：**make significant progress in …**

## 7. 翻译题材词库：文化、科技、交通、社会、教育

### 7.1 中国文化与传统

| 中文           | 英文表达                           | 常用搭配/提醒                                                  |
|----------------|------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|

## 8. 高频句型与可替换例句

### 8.1 not only … but also …（不仅……而且……）

- Digital tools not only improve efficiency but also make learning
  resources more accessible.
- Social practice not only develops practical skills but also deepens
  students’ understanding of society.
- Traditional customs not only preserve cultural memory but also
  strengthen a sense of identity.

### 8.2 By doing …, …（通过做某事……）

- By taking part in internships, students can gain practical experience.
- By checking several reliable sources, readers can reduce the risk of
  being misled.
- By setting realistic goals, students can manage their time more
  effectively.

### 8.3 enable sb. to do sth.（使某人能够做某事）

- Online resources enable students to learn at their own pace.
- Practical projects enable learners to apply knowledge to real
  problems.
- Reliable public transport enables residents to travel more
  conveniently.

### 8.4 make it possible for sb. to do sth.（使某人做某事成为可能）

- Digital technology makes it possible for people to access services
  remotely.
- Better infrastructure makes it possible for goods to move more
  efficiently.

### 8.5 contribute to + 名词/动名词（有助于、促成）

- Regular reading contributes to the development of critical thinking.
- Teamwork contributes to building trust among group members.
- Public investment contributes to improving local services.

### 8.6 play an important role in + 名词/动名词（在……中发挥重要作用）

- Basic knowledge plays an important role in further learning.
- Digital literacy plays an important role in identifying misleading
  information.
- Public transportation plays an important role in urban development.

### 8.7 be regarded as + 名词（被视为……）

- Independent thinking should be regarded as an essential learning
  ability.
- The fan is regarded as both a practical object and a form of art.

### 8.8 be widely used/applied in + 领域（被广泛用于……）

- Digital technology is widely used in education and public services.
- Satellite navigation systems are widely applied in transportation and
  disaster relief.

### 8.9 the more …, the more …（越……越……）

- The more students practise, the more confident they become.
- The more carefully people verify information, the less likely they are
  to spread false claims.
- The more experience students gain, the better prepared they will be
  for future challenges.

### 8.10 rather than（而不是）

- Students should learn to evaluate AI-generated answers rather than
  copy them directly.
- We should address the root cause rather than focus only on the
  symptoms.
- The aim is to develop independent thinking rather than memorize fixed
  answers.

### 8.11 while / although（虽然、而……）

- While technology offers convenience, it may also create new privacy
  risks.
- Although practical experience is valuable, it should complement rather
  than replace academic learning.

注意：**although 不要和 but 在同一句中重复使用**。例如不要写 *Although
it is useful, but it has risks.* 应写 *Although it is useful, it has
risks.* 或 *It is useful, but it has risks.*

## 9. 最容易用错的词与搭配

| 常见错误                              | 推荐写法                                                                           | 说明                                                |
|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| improve students’ communicate skills  | improve students’ communication skills                                             | communication 是名词，作定语修饰 skills             |
| improve the ability of communicate    | improve communication skills / improve the ability to communicate                  | ability 后常用 to do                                |
| contribute to improve                 | contribute to improving                                                            | 这里 to 是介词，后接名词或动名词                    |
| be benefit to                         | be beneficial to / benefit                                                         | beneficial 是形容词；benefit 可作动词/名词          |
| bring many convenience                | bring a lot of convenience / bring convenience to people                           | convenience 通常不可数                              |
| many informations                     | much information / a lot of information                                            | information 不可数                                  |
| many knowledges                       | much knowledge / pieces of knowledge                                               | knowledge 通常不可数                                |
| an important role for                 | play an important role in                                                          | 固定搭配 play a role in                             |
| pay attention for                     | pay attention to                                                                   | 固定搭配 to                                         |
| discuss about the problem             | discuss the problem                                                                | discuss 后直接接宾语，不需要 about                  |
| help students to improving            | help students improve / help students to improve                                   | help sb. (to) do sth.                               |
| make students can learn               | enable students to learn / make it possible for students to learn                  | 不要把 make 和 can 硬拼在一起                       |
| be used to do sth. / be used to doing | be used to do = 被用来做；be used to doing = 习惯于做                              | 两种结构意思不同                                    |
| one of the most important ability     | one of the most important abilities                                                | one of + 最高级 + 复数名词                          |
| the number of students are            | the number of students is                                                          | the number 作主语时谓语通常用单数                   |
| students needs to                     | students need to                                                                   | 复数主语配动词原形                                  |
| people can gets                       | people can get                                                                     | 情态动词 can 后接动词原形                           |
| an increasing number of information   | an increasing amount of information                                                | information 不可数，用 amount                       |
| social practice improve ability       | social practice improves ability / social practice can improve students’ abilities | 单数主语注意谓语形式；ability 根据语境决定单复数    |
| technology development                | technological development / the development of technology                          | technological 是形容词                              |
| traditional culture should be inherit | traditional culture should be inherited/preserved                                  | 被动语态需要过去分词                                |
| the system apply in transport         | the system is applied in transportation                                            | 被动语态：be applied in/to                          |
| one of the most dynamic region        | one of the most dynamic regions                                                    | region 要用复数                                     |
| it can make people improve            | it can help people improve / it can enable people to improve                       | make sb. do 可以，但 make people improve 语义要自然 |

### 低风险表达优先原则

如果你不确定高级词怎么搭配，优先用正确、自然的简单表达：

- improve skills \> 使用不确定的“高级”动词
- use technology wisely \> 用错 utilize / exploit
- solve a problem / address an issue \> 生造复杂表达
- help students develop … \> 不确定时不用过长的使役结构
- people living in poverty \> 不确定时不用复杂抽象名词

**六级作文不是越生僻越好。准确的动词 + 具体的例子 +
清楚的逻辑，通常比堆砌难词更有用。**

## 12. 怎样把这份资料真正用进考试

你不需要一次背完所有词。推荐先把高频动词和搭配变成自己的句子。

### 第一轮：先掌握最值得复用的 25 个词块

1.  improve skills / improve efficiency
2.  enhance awareness / enhance understanding
3.  boost confidence
4.  promote cooperation / promote development
5.  strengthen relationships / strengthen skills
6.  develop critical thinking
7.  put theory into practice
8.  gain practical experience
9.  take practical measures
10. address the problem
11. solve a problem
12. overcome challenges
13. bring benefits to
14. have a positive impact on
15. pose a risk to
16. over-reliance on AI
17. protect privacy
18. set realistic goals
19. adapt to change
20. strike a balance between A and B
21. make full use of resources
22. play an important role in
23. contribute to development
24. lay a solid foundation for
25. be better prepared for future challenges

### 第二轮：每个词块写一个自己的句子

不要只抄：

- 中文提示：实践能提高学生的沟通能力。
- 自己先写：Social practice can improve students’ communication skills.
- 再换主题：Group projects can improve students’ problem-solving skills.
- 再换动词：Group projects can strengthen students’ problem-solving
  skills.

这样你练到的是“词块迁移”，而不是只记住一条范文句子。

### 第三轮：每次写作只选 5—8 个表达

作文 150—200 词不需要堆满高级词。建议每篇有：

- 1 个清楚的开头句；
- 2—3 个论证动词词块；
- 1 个具体例子；
- 1 个让步/风险表达（如果题目适合）；
- 1 个落到行动的结尾句。

### 第四轮：翻译按“主语—动词—信息”拆句

例如：

中文：北斗系统被广泛应用于多个领域，为用户提供高精度定位服务。

1.  主语：The BeiDou system
2.  动词：is widely used/applied
3.  范围：in many fields
4.  补充信息：and provides high-precision positioning services to users.

合成：**The BeiDou system is widely used in many fields and provides
high-precision positioning services to users.**

中文：传统习俗体现了中国文化的独特魅力。

1.  主语：Traditional customs
2.  动词：reflect/show
3.  宾语：the unique charm of Chinese culture

合成：**Traditional customs reflect the unique charm of Chinese
culture.**

### 最后记住

**词汇不是越多越好，而是要能在正确的地方用出来。** 优先熟练掌握 improve
/ enhance / boost / promote / strengthen / develop，以及 practice /
benefit / drawback / risk / address / overcome
这些能覆盖很多题目的核心词。每次从范文中只摘出真正能复用的表达，再通过新主题重写一遍，才算掌握。

## 13. 2016—2025 年公开参考范文：逐年好句、词块与用法

### 2016 年 12 月：发明的价值与鼓励创新

|---|---|---|---|

### 14.2 翻译最值得记的动词词族

### 14.3 翻译题高频句架（按真实题材归纳）

以下句架是**根据历年翻译题材归纳的可迁移结构**，不是逐字原文。

1. **地理位置：** `X is located in the southern/northern part of Y.` — X 位于 Y 的南/北部。
2. **面积/规模：** `X covers an area of ... square kilometers.` — X 占地……平方公里。
3. **历史：** `X has a history of more than ... years.` — X 有……多年的历史。
4. **被称为：** `X is known as ...` / `X is often referred to as ...` — X 被称为……。
5. **以……闻名：** `X is famous/known for ...` — X 以……闻名。
6. **体现价值：** `X reflects the cultural values of ...` — X 体现……的文化价值。
7. **被广泛应用：** `X is widely used/applied in ...` — X 被广泛应用于……。
8. **促进发展：** `X has contributed to the development of ...` — X 促进了……的发展。
9. **改善生活：** `X has improved local residents’ living standards.` — X 改善了当地居民的生活水平。
10. **带来便利：** `X brings convenience to local residents.` — X 给当地居民带来便利。
11. **减少时间：** `X reduces the time needed to travel from A to B.` — X 缩短了从 A 到 B 的出行时间。
12. **提供服务：** `X provides ... services to users/residents.` — X 为用户/居民提供……服务。
13. **吸引游客：** `X attracts a large number of tourists every year.` — X 每年吸引大量游客。
14. **保护生态：** `Measures have been taken to protect the local ecosystem.` — 已采取措施保护当地生态系统。
15. **在……中发挥作用：** `X plays an important role in ...` — X 在……中发挥重要作用。
16. **结果状语：** `..., thereby improving ...` — ……，从而改善……。
17. **定语从句：** `X, which is known for ..., ...` — X 以……闻名，它……。
18. **非谓语压缩：** `..., attracting visitors from home and abroad.` — ……，吸引国内外游客。

### 14.4 翻译参考译文里常见的词，别逐词硬套

| 中文 | 推荐写法 | 常见错误/提醒 |
|---|---|---|
| 位居……之首 | rank first among... / top... | 不要写 `sit at first place`。 |
| 源于 | originate in/from... | originate in 常接地区/地方；be derived from 常强调来源。 |
| 不畏严寒 | brave the cold / withstand the cold | 不要直译成 `not afraid of cold`，除非语境很口语。 |
| 迎着风雪绽放 | bloom in the snow and wind / bloom despite the cold | 可按语义选择，不必追求逐字对应。 |
| 获取灵感 | gain/draw inspiration from... | `inspiration` 通常作不可数名词。 |
| 定为市花 | designate X as the city flower | `designate A as B` 是固定结构。 |
| 占地 | cover an area of... | 面积表达常用 `cover an area of`。 |
| 平均海拔 | an average altitude/elevation of... | altitude/elevation 都可表示海拔，需看语境。 |
| 生态修复 | ecological restoration | restoration 是名词；restore 是动词。 |
| 城市治理 | urban governance | 不建议直译为 `city manage`。 |
| 高品质生活环境 | a high-quality living environment | `quality` 是名词，`high-quality` 作复合形容词。 |
| 资源循环利用 | resource recycling / the recycling of resources | 视句子结构选择名词短语或动词结构。 |
| 节约型社会 | a conservation-minded society / a resource-conserving society | 根据上下文选自然表达，不必只认一个版本。 |

## 15. 同义替换总表补强：你点名的高频中文词

这部分集中回应“一个高频词要有多个同义替换，放在一起记”的需求。词汇应当连同搭配一起学，不要只记一排英文。

### 15.1 “提高/进步/增强”全词族

| 英文 | 中文侧重点 | 最常用搭配 | 六级可用例句（教学改写） |
|---|---|---|---|
| improve | 改善、提高，最通用 | improve skills/quality/efficiency | Regular practice can improve students’ writing skills. |
| enhance | 提升质量、能力、意识 | enhance ability/awareness/confidence/employability | Internships can enhance students’ employability. |
| boost | 提振、推动指标明显上升 | boost confidence/motivation/productivity | Small achievements can boost confidence. |
| strengthen | 加强已有的能力、关系、基础 | strengthen cooperation/skills/ties | Team projects can strengthen cooperation. |
| raise | 提高数值、标准、意识 | raise awareness/standards/funds | Schools should raise students’ awareness of online safety. |
| increase | 增加数量、程度 | increase the number/level/rate | The program can increase access to learning resources. |
| develop | 逐步培养、发展 | develop skills/habits/ability | Students should develop independent thinking. |
| foster | 培养某种品质、氛围、关系 | foster creativity/trust/independence | Education should foster creativity. |
| cultivate | 有意识地培养习惯、品质 | cultivate self-discipline/empathy | Students need to cultivate good study habits. |
| advance | 推进事业、技术、研究 | advance science/research/progress | Innovation can advance scientific research. |
| promote | 促进某领域发展、推广理念 | promote health/cooperation/development | Public transport can promote sustainable development. |
| refine | 使想法、方法、技能更成熟 | refine ideas/methods/skills | Feedback helps learners refine their writing. |
| optimize | 优化流程、资源、系统 | optimize processes/resources | Digital tools can optimize the learning process. |
| upgrade | 升级设备、系统、设施 | upgrade infrastructure/systems | The city plans to upgrade its transport system. |
| make progress | 取得进步 | make progress in... | Students make progress through consistent practice. |
| make an improvement | 做出改善 | make improvements to... | The school made improvements to its online learning platform. |
| achieve progress | 取得进展，使用范围相对窄 | achieve progress in... | The project achieved progress in energy conservation. |

**最重要的区别：** `improve` 可以作为默认选择；`enhance` 常与能力/质量/意识搭配；`boost` 常与信心/动力/生产率搭配；`raise` 与 `increase` 关注数量或水平；`strengthen` 是把已有的东西加强；`foster/cultivate` 是逐渐培养；`promote` 是促进某种发展。`progress` 通常作不可数名词，常见表达是 `make progress`，不是随意写 `many progresses`。
